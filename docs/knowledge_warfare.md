
### **"Knowledge Warfare Engine"**

Build a **platform** that does multimodal reasoning across institutional memory, but **demo it in ONE vertical where the pain is visceral and visual**. The trick is showing the platform is **domain-agnostic** while your demo is **domain-deep**.

#### **Your Architecture (Universal)**
```
Messy Input Pipeline → Gemini 3 Reasoning Layer → Strategic Output Layer
   ↓                          ↓                         ↓
Scans, audio, video,     Chain-of-thought          Not Q&A—
Slack, code, CAD,        inference, conflict       DECISION
handwritten notes,       detection, temporal       SUPPORT
architectural diagrams   reasoning, attribution
```

#### **Your Demo (Specific & Jaw-Dropping)**
Pick **ONE** of these to showcase the engine's power:

**Option A: Legal Discovery (Conflict Forensics)**
- **Input**: 500-page acquisition contract (PDF) + partner's handwritten sticky notes (photo) + Slack thread about "special indemnity"
- **Wow Moment**: System overlays the contract, *reasons* that the handwritten note creates an *unintentional waiver* of a key clause, cross-references it with 3 past cases, flags **privilege risk**, and generates a "strategic exposure report" with **multimodal citations** (e.g., "Conflict identified at 73% confidence: Sticky note annotation on page 47 contradicts email timestamp 03/15 11:42pm").

**Option B: Pharma R&D (Protocol Deviation)**
- **Input**: Lab notebook scan (coffee stains) + Zoom recording of scientist explaining results + experimental data CSV
- **Wow Moment**: System identifies that the *handwritten* protocol deviation (circled in the scan) wasn't documented in the ELN, **reasons** it invalidates the conclusion, and auto-generates FDA compliance risk assessment linking the audio confession to the data anomaly.

**Option C: M&A Due Diligence (Hidden Liability)**
- **Input**: 10,000-doc data room (PPTs, emails, contracts) + voice memo from CFO + architecture diagram showing data flow
- **Wow Moment**: System **infers** a GDPR violation that *no single document states explicitly* by reasoning across the diagram (data flows to third party), contract (no DPA clause), and email (IT's concern about "legacy access").

- **Innovation (30%)**: "Multimodal reasoning engine" ≠ chatbot. You're using Gemini 3's **reasoning** and **multimodal** superpowers.

- **Technical (40%)**: Complex ingestion pipeline + reasoning layer + attribution system = serious engineering.
- **Presentation (10%)**: The **30-second domain switch** at the end of your video is the mic drop.

**Bottom line**: Don't limit to legal in the **product vision**, but absolutely **demo with legal** (or pharma) to make the abstract concrete. The platform is horizontal; the proof is vertical.
